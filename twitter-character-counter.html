Twitter/X character limits explained

Twitter/X allows up to 280 characters per tweet — doubled from the original 140-character limit introduced in 2017. URLs, regardless of length, are wrapped by Twitter's t.co shortener and count as exactly 23 characters. Your bio is limited to 160 characters, which is enough for two or three short sentences. The display name (the bold name shown above your @handle) is capped at 50 characters.

How to make the most of 280 characters

Short tweets (under 100 characters) historically get more engagement because they leave room for a quoted reply. Front-load your most important point — the first 20–30 characters are what appears in notifications and link previews. Emoji count as 2 characters each on some platforms, though Twitter/X counts most as 1. When in doubt, count before you post.

When does character count matter most?

Character count matters most when drafting tweets in a document, planning a thread in advance, or writing a bio without being logged in. Drafting offline avoids the frustration of having Twitter silently cut your text when pasting. This counter gives you an accurate character count before you ever open the app.
