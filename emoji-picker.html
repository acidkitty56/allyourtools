Emoji Picker

Click any emoji to instantly copy it to your clipboard. Search or filter by category.

How to use the emoji picker

Simply click on any emoji in the grid and it will be instantly copied to your clipboard. A toast notification will confirm the copy. You can then paste the emoji anywhere — in a social media post, chat message, email, document, or website. Use the category filter buttons to browse specific types of emojis, or type in the search box to find emojis by name.

Emoji categories

This picker includes emojis organized into six categories: Smileys (face expressions and emotions), Animals (mammals, birds, bugs, and sea creatures), Food (fruits, vegetables, meals, and drinks), Travel (vehicles, landmarks, and locations), Objects (technology, tools, and everyday items), and Symbols (hearts, stars, shapes, and abstract icons).

Why use an emoji picker?

While most smartphones have built-in emoji keyboards, desktop computers often make emoji insertion cumbersome. Operating system emoji pickers (Windows Key + . on Windows, Control + Command + Space on Mac) exist but can be slow or hard to find. This web-based picker is always available in any browser, loads instantly, and lets you search by name rather than scrolling through thousands of emojis.

Emoji compatibility

Emojis are standardized through the Unicode Consortium, which means most modern emojis render consistently across major platforms including iOS, Android, Windows, and macOS. Older emojis (included in Unicode 6.0 and earlier) are virtually universal. Newer emojis may display differently or appear as blank boxes on older operating systems or browsers.
