Lorem Ipsum Generator

Generate placeholder text for designs, mockups, and layouts — choose paragraphs, sentences, or words.

What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem ipsum is dummy text that has been the standard placeholder in typesetting and graphic design since the 1500s. It is derived from "de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (On the Ends of Good and Evil) by Cicero, written in 45 BC. The scrambled Latin text is intentionally meaningless so that viewers focus on the visual design — layout, fonts, spacing — rather than reading the words. The classic opening "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet…" has become instantly recognisable to designers worldwide.

Why use placeholder text?

Placeholder text fills space during the design and prototyping phase so you can evaluate visual hierarchy, line lengths, column widths, and typography before real content is ready. Using actual content too early can distract stakeholders from giving layout feedback. Lorem ipsum is also useful for testing rendering engines, checking that a UI handles long text gracefully, and populating demo databases with realistic-looking text data.

How this tool works

Choose between paragraphs, sentences, or words from the dropdown, set the desired amount, and click Generate. The tool picks random words from a classic lorem ipsum word list and assembles them into sentences of 8–15 words each, with the first word capitalised and a period at the end. Paragraphs group 3–6 sentences together. The first generated paragraph always starts with the traditional "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit." opening. Everything runs in your browser — no server, no tracking.

Common use cases

Fill wireframe mockups and design comps with realistic-looking body text. Populate email templates with sample content for client reviews. Test responsive text layouts across different screen sizes. Generate test data for CMS content fields or database seeding scripts. Quickly produce multi-paragraph copy for presentations and slide decks when real content is pending.
