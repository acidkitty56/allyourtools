Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Introduction

Welcome to All Your Tools (allyourtools.net). We take your privacy seriously. This page explains what information is collected when you use this website, how it is used, and what choices you have.

The short version: this website does not collect your personal data directly. The tools run entirely in your browser — nothing you type or generate is sent to our servers.

Information We Collect

All Your Tools does not collect, store, or process personal information such as your name, email address, or any input you provide to the tools. Every tool on this site runs client-side — entirely within your browser — and no results or inputs are transmitted to any server operated by us.

We may collect basic, anonymised technical data through analytics services (such as page views and session counts) to understand how the site is used and to improve the experience. This data cannot be used to identify individual users.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. This site may use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Functionality: to remember preferences such as your last-used tool settings.
  • Analytics: to understand aggregate traffic patterns and improve the site. Analytics data is anonymised and not tied to individual users.
  • Advertising: third-party advertising partners (see below) may set cookies to serve relevant ads.

You can disable or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect some site functionality. Instructions for managing cookies can be found in your browser's help section.

Third-Party Advertising

All Your Tools may display advertisements served by third-party advertising partners. These partners may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to show you relevant ads based on your browsing activity.

We do not control the data practices of these third-party services. Their use of cookies is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one. You are encouraged to review their policies directly.

Google AdSense

This site may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense is an advertising service provided by Google LLC. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other sites on the internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

For more information on how Google uses data, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms.

Data Protection

Because All Your Tools does not collect personal data directly, there is no personal information held by us to access, correct, or delete. All tool inputs remain on your device and are processed entirely in your browser.

For any data that third-party services may collect (such as Google Analytics or Google AdSense), please refer to their respective privacy policies and use their opt-out mechanisms to exercise your rights.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. We recommend checking back periodically if you have concerns about privacy.

Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please reach out via the About page. We will do our best to respond promptly.
