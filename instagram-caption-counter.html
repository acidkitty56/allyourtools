Instagram character limits explained

Instagram allows up to 2,200 characters in a post caption, making it possible to write detailed, article-style content. However, Instagram truncates captions after approximately 125 characters on the feed view, showing a "more" link. Only the first 125 characters are visible without tapping — so your opening line is critical. Your profile bio is limited to just 150 characters, and usernames can be up to 30 characters.

Writing captions that work within the limit

The most effective Instagram captions put the hook in the first line, before the fold. Use the remaining 2,000+ characters for context, storytelling, hashtags, and calls to action. Hashtags count toward your character limit — 30 hashtags of average length consume around 400 characters. Many creators add hashtags in a comment rather than the caption itself to keep the caption clean.

Bio character limit tips

With only 150 characters, your Instagram bio needs to be tight. Include your value proposition, a keyword for discoverability, and a call to action pointing to your link in bio — all within the limit. Line breaks count as characters, so use them sparingly. This counter lets you draft and refine your bio offline before committing it to your profile.
