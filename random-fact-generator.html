Random Fact Generator

Discover fascinating facts about science, history, animals, technology, and space — click for a new one any time.

Why learn random facts?

Random facts are a fantastic way to satisfy curiosity, spark conversations, and keep your mind engaged. They bridge knowledge gaps between disciplines — a fact about animal biology might connect to evolutionary theory, which connects to geology. The "did you know?" facts we remember best tend to be the ones that challenge our assumptions or reveal something surprising about the world we thought we understood.

About our fact categories

Science: Physics, chemistry, biology, and earth science — the fundamental laws that govern our universe. History: Little-known events, surprising timelines, and myths debunked. Animals: The extraordinary capabilities and surprising behaviours of the creatures we share the planet with. Technology: The origins, quirks, and scale of the tools and systems that define modern life. Space: The staggering scales, bizarre physics, and beautiful weirdness of the cosmos.

How facts are selected

All facts in our collection are curated for accuracy, interest, and accessibility. We prioritise facts that are verifiable, genuinely surprising, and not widely known. The generator randomly selects from the current category's pool, avoiding consecutive repeats so each click brings something new. With 80+ facts across five categories, you can explore for quite a while before seeing a repeat.

Using facts in conversation and content

Facts make great conversation starters, icebreakers, and social media content. Use the "Copy Fact" button to grab any fact and paste it wherever you need it. Facts are particularly effective in presentations, educational content, and trivia nights. A well-chosen fact can completely reframe how people think about a topic — making it a powerful tool for teachers, writers, and public speakers.
