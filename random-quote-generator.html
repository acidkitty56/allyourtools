Random Quote Generator

Get inspired — or amused — with a random quote from 50+ famous thinkers, leaders, and comedians.

Why quotes matter

A well-chosen quote can shift perspective, provide comfort, inspire action, or simply make you laugh. Quotes distill wisdom, humor, or insight into a few memorable words. Throughout history, the most powerful ideas have often been crystallized into short, quotable phrases that spread and endure. From Aristotle to Einstein, from Churchill to Oscar Wilde, the right words at the right moment can be transformative.

What's included

This generator contains over 50 curated quotes spanning inspirational, motivational, philosophical, and humorous categories. Authors include Steve Jobs, Albert Einstein, Nelson Mandela, Eleanor Roosevelt, Winston Churchill, Maya Angelou, Oscar Wilde, Mark Twain, Mahatma Gandhi, Walt Disney, and many more. The selection is intentionally diverse — mixing serious wisdom with lighter wit.

How to use the quotes

Click "New Quote" to get a random quote — each click guarantees a different quote from the previous one. Click "Copy Quote" to copy both the quote text and attribution to your clipboard, ready to paste into a message, social post, email, or document. The quotes include Unicode curly quotation marks and an em dash before the author's name, so they look polished wherever you paste them.

Uses for random quotes

Random quote generators are used for daily inspiration, social media content, presentations, email signatures, creative writing prompts, and icebreaker activities. Teachers use them to start class discussions. Writers use them as creative prompts. Developers use them for placeholder content in apps and mockups. Sometimes the perfect quote for a situation is just one click away.
