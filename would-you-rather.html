What is Would You Rather?

Would You Rather is a classic party game and conversation starter where players choose between two hypothetical options, each with its own trade-offs. The game is designed to spark debate, reveal preferences, and get people talking. It works equally well as an icebreaker in professional settings, a family dinner game, or a way to kill time with friends.

How to play

Click "Next Question" to get a new dilemma. Read both options and click the one you prefer to cast your vote — you'll see a simulated percentage showing how others chose. There are no right or wrong answers; the whole point is to justify your choice and hear what others think. The best Would You Rather sessions often end in long, spirited debates about edge cases and hypotheticals.

Would You Rather in group settings

This game is perfect for groups — use the "Copy Question" button to share the current question with your chat group, over video call, or on screen during a team event. Everyone votes simultaneously (by holding up fingers, using a poll, or just shouting), and then the discussion begins. Questions about superpowers, lifestyle choices, and hypothetical dilemmas tend to generate the most engagement.

Variations of the game

There are many variations: "Would You Rather — Gross Edition" with disgusting options; "Would You Rather — Philosophical Edition" with deep ethical dilemmas; and "Would You Rather — Funny Edition" with absurd scenarios. Our collection tries to balance thought-provoking, fun, and imaginative questions suitable for all ages.
