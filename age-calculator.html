How the age calculator works

The calculator subtracts your date of birth from today's date using calendar arithmetic. It accounts for varying month lengths and leap years, so the result in months and days is always accurate. The calculation runs entirely in your browser — no data is sent to any server. The result updates immediately when you select or change your birth date.

Years, months, and days explained

Age in years is straightforward: how many complete calendar years have passed since your birth date. Months counts the additional full months beyond the last birthday. Days counts the remaining days in the current partial month. For example, if you were born on March 5, 1990, and today is November 18, 2025, your age is 35 years, 8 months, and 13 days.

Next birthday countdown

The birthday countdown shows how many days remain until your next birthday. It always counts forward to the upcoming date regardless of what month you are in. If your birthday falls today, the tool will display a celebration message. The countdown is useful for planning birthday events, sending cards, or simply satisfying curiosity.

Uses for an age calculator

Age calculators are commonly used for eligibility checks (voting age, retirement age, age-restricted services), medical contexts (pediatric age milestones), legal documents requiring exact age at a specific date, and fun statistics like calculating age in days or determining what day of the week you were born. Schools and HR departments also use age calculations for enrollment cutoffs and benefits eligibility.
