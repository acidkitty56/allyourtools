Random Joke Generator

Get a fresh random joke from 60+ jokes. Choose a category or go all-in with the full mix.

Why jokes matter

Laughter is a universal human experience that reduces stress, builds social bonds, and improves mood. Studies have shown that humour activates the brain's reward system and releases endorphins. Whether you need an icebreaker, a pick-me-up, or something to share with friends and colleagues, a good joke is always welcome.

About the categories

Programming jokes are beloved in developer culture — they rely on knowledge of coding, debugging, and computer science concepts. Dad jokes are intentionally groan-worthy puns that are so bad they loop back around to being good. Puns play on multiple meanings of a word or similar-sounding words. One-liners deliver the entire joke in a single sentence for maximum efficiency.

How jokes are selected

Each time you click "Next Joke," a random joke is selected from the current category's pool. The generator avoids repeating the most recent joke, so you won't see the same joke twice in a row. With 60+ jokes in the library and categories to filter by, you can keep clicking for a long time before seeing a repeat.

Sharing jokes

Use the "Copy Joke" button to copy the current joke to your clipboard, ready to paste into a chat message, email, or social media post. Jokes are great for breaking the ice in presentations, lightening the mood in team meetings, or just making someone's day a little brighter.
